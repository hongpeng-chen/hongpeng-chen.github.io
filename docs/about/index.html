About

Hongpeng Chen

Ph.D. researcher working on robot learning, manipulation, and embodied AI.

Biography

I am a Ph.D. researcher in Industrial and Systems Engineering at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, advised by Prof. Pai Zheng. In 2026, I am a visiting Ph.D. researcher in robot learning at Idiap Research Institute / EPFL, advised by Prof. Sylvain Calinon.

My research combines learning from demonstration, multimodal planning, and safe skill adaptation. I work across perception, planning, and control, with experience on physical manipulation platforms and simulation benchmarks.
